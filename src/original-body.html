HOW THE PRESS RUNS

Five stages, one continuous cycle.

01

Compound Mixing

Raw rubber and additives are milled on the two-roll mill to a uniform sheet ready for the press.

02

Preform & Load

Preforms are cut to shot weight and loaded into the heated mold cavity or transfer pot.

03

Molding

The press closes under rated tonnage, forcing compound into the cavity under heat and pressure.

04

Curing

Programmable dwell timers hold pressure and temperature for the vulcanization cycle set per compound.

05

Finishing & QC

Parts are deflashed, inspected against tolerance, and batch-logged before dispatch.
